Spend Compliance & Savings Monitor

Blueprint AI · Wave 1 agentic product. Each week the agent checks every PO line at every unit against the formulary and trade agreements. It investigates what it finds, gets a named reviewer's approval, asks units to fix their POs, follows up, and confirms the saving once the PO is revised. People approve; the agent does the chasing.
